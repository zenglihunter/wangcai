RS Product Search

Search router-switch.com product data stored in the local Qdrant collection `rs-product` and return concise product candidates. Use when the user asks to find, recommend, compare, or look up products by brand, type, SKU, model, or use case, such as '推荐 Cisco 路由器', '找 Juniper license', '有没有 400GE switch', or '查 SKU XXX'.
